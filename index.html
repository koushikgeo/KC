About

Research and teaching at the intersection of rural development, environmental studies, human geography and participatory research.

Profile

I am an integrated geographer with expertise in socio-ecological systems research. I employ mixed-methods approaches combining qualitative, quantitative and participatory methods to understand intricate human–environment interactions.

My research interests are centred on sustainability sciences, including sustainable resource management, climate adaptation and nature-based solutions.

Research

Selected themes emerging across my research projects and publications.

Water Resources

Traditional water bodies, water governance, household water insecurity, waterscape values and sustainable water-resource management.

Urban Sustainability

Blue infrastructure, urban sustainability and the relationships between people, waterscapes and urban environments.

Climate Adaptation

Climate-change perceptions, adaptation strategies, rainwater harvesting and desired forms of institutional and government support.

Landscape Governance

Values, rules, knowledge, justice and governance in agricultural heritage landscapes and rapidly changing socio-ecological systems.

Experience

Research Experience

March 2023 – March 2025

Postdoctoral Fellow

Alexander von Humboldt Research Fellowship, Social-Ecological Interactions in Agricultural Systems, University of Kassel, Germany

Conducted interdisciplinary research on blue infrastructure and nature-based solutions for urban sustainability, examining human–waterscape relationships across Indian megacities through comparative and mixed-methods approaches. The work integrated household surveys, stakeholder engagement, quantitative analysis and qualitative research.

January – December 2022

Research Associate

Department of Industrial & Systems Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India

Conducted interdisciplinary research on the socio-economic impacts of COVID-19-induced migration in the Sundarbans region. The research combined geospatial analysis with field-based methods, including household surveys, focus group discussions, key informant interviews and case studies, supported by quantitative and statistical analysis.

August 2014 – February 2015

Research Associate

Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India

Investigated the sustainable use and management of traditional water-harvesting systems in Odisha and West Bengal, with a focus on their contribution to water security and climate-change adaptation. The research involved field-based data collection, stakeholder engagement, focus group discussions and key informant interviews, complemented by quantitative and multivariate analysis.
